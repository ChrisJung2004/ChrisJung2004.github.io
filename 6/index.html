Why The 30-Day Route?

Traveling is a lot of fun but planning is often the hardest part. This site takes the pressure out of planning by giving you a curated, month-long route based on the type of trip you want. You don't need to already know where you want to go.

How it works

  1. Pick the kind of trip you want.
  2. Pick a region that fits it.
  3. Get a month-long route with where to go, how long to stay, and where to sleep.
