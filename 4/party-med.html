Mediterranean Islands

Four islands with the biggest summer party scene in Europe. You start at the superclubs in Ibiza, then go to the beach clubs on the Greek islands, and finish on Hvar in Croatia. The days are for beaches and boat trips and the nights don't really start until midnight.

Route order: fly into Ibiza, fly to Mykonos (through Athens), take the ferry to Ios, then fly from Athens to Split and take the ferry to Hvar.
Stop Nights Highlights
Ibiza, Spain 8 Superclubs such as Hï Ibiza, Ushuaïa, Amnesia and Pacha; sunset at Café del Mar in Sant Antoni; quiet coves like Cala Comte
Mykonos, Greece 7 Paradise and Super Paradise beach clubs; sunset drinks in Little Venice; the famous windmills
Ios, Greece 7 Mylopotas beach; Far Out Beach Club; the bar crawl through the tiny lanes of Chora
Hvar, Croatia 8 Carpe Diem beach club on the Pakleni Islands; sunset at Hula Hula Hvar; a boat day around the Pakleni Islands
Total 30 4 stops

Where to stay

  • Ibiza: hostels and budget hotels in Sant Antoni, close to the clubs' bus routes
  • Mykonos: campsite cabins at Paradise Beach are the cheapest beds on the island
  • Ios: party hostels in Chora or right on Mylopotas beach
  • Hvar: hostels in Hvar Town, a short walk from the harbor taxis

At a glance

Best time: June to September. Ibiza's opening and closing parties fall in early June and early October.

Daily budget: About €90–150 per person per day. Club entry in Ibiza alone can run €50–100.

Getting around: Ferries between Greek islands (Blue Star, SeaJets) and short budget flights between countries.

Fly in / fly out: Arrive in Ibiza (IBZ) and leave from Split (SPU).
