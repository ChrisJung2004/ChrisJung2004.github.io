Ski trips

These routes are a full month of skiing, with a different mountain about every week. Each stop is connected by train, bus, or a short flight so you don't need a car. The first three routes are best from December to April, and the Andes route is for June to September when it is winter down there.

Step 2: Pick a region

  • The Alps Chamonix, Zermatt, St. Anton, Dolomites Best time: Dec–Apr
  • Nordic Countries Winter slopes under the northern lights Levi, Åre, Hemsedal Best time: Dec–Apr
  • East Asia Japan & Korea powder Niseko, Hakuba, Pyeongchang Best time: Dec–Mar
  • The Andes Southern Hemisphere ski season Portillo, Valle Nevado, Bariloche Best time: Jun–Sep
