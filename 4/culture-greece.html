Greece & Turkey

This route is where Europe meets Asia. You walk up to the Acropolis, watch the sunset over the volcano in Santorini, and ride a hot air balloon over Cappadocia. Then you finish in Istanbul, which was the capital of two different empires.

Route order: fly into Athens, ferry to Santorini, fly to Cappadocia (through Istanbul), and finish in Istanbul.
Stop Nights Highlights
Athens, Greece 6 The Acropolis and Parthenon; the Acropolis Museum; souvlaki in Plaka
Santorini, Greece 6 Sunset in Oia; the caldera hike from Fira to Oia (about 10 km); volcanic-soil wineries
Cappadocia, Turkey 7 A sunrise hot-air balloon flight; the Göreme Open-Air Museum's cave churches; the Derinkuyu underground city; sleeping in a cave hotel
Istanbul, Turkey 11 Hagia Sophia; the Blue Mosque; the Grand Bazaar; a Bosphorus ferry ride; kebabs, simit and baklava
Total 30 4 stops

Where to stay

  • Athens: hostels in Psyrri or Plaka, below the Acropolis
  • Santorini: hostels in Fira or Perissa; Oia is the most expensive village
  • Cappadocia: cave hotels in Göreme
  • Istanbul: hostels in Sultanahmet or Karaköy

At a glance

Best time: April to June or September to October, warm but without the summer crowds and heat.

Daily budget: About €70–120 per person per day. Santorini is the most expensive stop; Turkey is much cheaper.

Getting around: Ferries in Greece and domestic flights in Turkey (Turkish Airlines, Pegasus).

Fly in / fly out: Arrive in Athens (ATH) and leave from Istanbul (IST).
