East Asia: China

Palaces, the Great Wall, the Terracotta Army, and giant pandas, all connected by one of the fastest train systems in the world. Every stop has its own food too, from Peking duck in Beijing to Sichuan hotpot in Chengdu to soup dumplings in Shanghai.

Route order: fly into Beijing, then take high-speed trains to Xi'an, Chengdu and Shanghai, and fly home from Shanghai.
Stop Nights Highlights
Beijing 8 The Great Wall at Mutianyu; the Forbidden City; Peking duck; cycling the old hutong alleys
Xi'an 6 The Terracotta Army; street food in the Muslim Quarter; biking on top of the ancient city wall
Chengdu 7 Giant pandas at the Chengdu Research Base; Sichuan hotpot and mapo tofu; a day trip to the Leshan Giant Buddha
Shanghai 9 The Bund skyline at night; Yu Garden; xiaolongbao (soup dumplings); the canal town of Zhujiajiao
Total 30 4 stops

Where to stay

  • Beijing: courtyard hostels in the hutongs
  • Xi'an: hostels inside the city wall
  • Chengdu: hostels near Kuanzhai Alley
  • Shanghai: hostels near the Bund or People's Square

At a glance

Best time: April to May or September to October. Avoid the Golden Week holiday (October 1–7), when the whole country travels.

Daily budget: About $50–90 per person per day.

Getting around: High-speed trains between every city; metros within cities. Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before you go, since cash is rarely used.

Fly in / fly out: Arrive in Beijing (PEK) and leave from Shanghai (PVG).
