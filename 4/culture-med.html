Western Mediterranean

This is the best of European art and food. You see ancient Rome, Renaissance Florence, and the museums and bakeries of Paris. Then you finish in San Sebastián, a beach town in Basque Country with some of the best food in Spain.

Route order: fly into Rome, take the train to Florence, fly or train to Paris, then take the TGV south to the Spanish border and fly home from Bilbao.
Stop Nights Highlights
Rome, Italy 8 The Colosseum and Roman Forum; the Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel; carbonara and cacio e pepe in Trastevere
Florence, Italy 7 The Uffizi Gallery; climbing Brunelleschi's Duomo; bistecca alla fiorentina; a Chianti wine day trip
Paris, France 8 The Louvre and Musée d'Orsay; Montmartre; croissants and baguettes from neighborhood bakeries
San Sebastián, Spain 7 A pintxos (Basque tapas) crawl in the Old Town; La Concha beach; a traditional cider house dinner
Total 30 4 stops

Where to stay

  • Rome: hostels near Termini station or in Trastevere
  • Florence: hostels near Santa Maria Novella station
  • Paris: hostels in the Marais or Canal Saint-Martin area
  • San Sebastián: guesthouses in the Old Town, steps from the pintxos bars

At a glance

Best time: April to June or September to October. August is hot, crowded, and many Paris restaurants close.

Daily budget: About €100–160 per person per day. Paris is the most expensive stop.

Getting around: Trains (Trenitalia, Italo, TGV) and one short flight. Book high-speed trains early for the best prices.

Fly in / fly out: Arrive in Rome (FCO) and leave from Bilbao (BIO).
