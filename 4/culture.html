Culture / Food trips

These routes are about history, cities, and food. You will see ruins, museums, and some of the best food in the world. The pace is a little slower so you have time for markets, cooking classes, and long dinners.

  • Greece & Turkey Athens, Santorini, Cappadocia, Istanbul Best time: Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct
  • Western Mediterranean Rome, Florence, Paris, San Sebastián Best time: Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct
