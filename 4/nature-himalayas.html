The Himalayas

The biggest mountains in the world. This route is built around two of the best treks in Nepal, Everest Base Camp and Poon Hill in the Annapurnas. Then you fly to Ladakh in India, which is a high desert that people call "Little Tibet." The altitude is no joke, so the plan includes rest days to get used to it.

Route order: fly into Kathmandu, fly to Lukla for the trek, bus or fly to Pokhara, then fly Kathmandu to Delhi to Leh and home from Delhi.
Stop Nights Highlights
Kathmandu, Nepal 3 Durbar Square; the Boudhanath stupa; momo dumplings; picking up trekking permits
Everest Base Camp trek, Nepal 14 The flight into Lukla; Namche Bazaar acclimatization days; Tengboche Monastery; sunrise over Everest from Kala Patthar
Pokhara & Annapurna, Nepal 6 Phewa Lake; the Poon Hill trek for sunrise over the Annapurna range; paragliding over the lake
Leh, Ladakh, India 7 Thiksey Monastery; Pangong Tso lake; the Khardung La mountain pass. Spend your first two days resting at altitude

Where to stay

  • Kathmandu: guesthouses in Thamel, the trekkers' district
  • Everest trek: teahouses (simple lodges) in each village along the trail
  • Pokhara: lakeside guesthouses
  • Leh: family-run guesthouses in the old town

At a glance

Best time: Autumn (October to November) is the clearest. Spring (March to May) brings rhododendron blooms. Visit Ladakh in the autumn window, before the passes close.

Daily budget: About $35–60 per person per day, plus trekking permits and a guide. Nepal now requires most foreign trekkers in its protected areas to hire a licensed guide.

Getting around: Domestic flights (Lukla, Pokhara, Leh), tourist buses, and walking.

Fly in / fly out: Arrive in Kathmandu (KTM) and leave from Delhi (DEL).
