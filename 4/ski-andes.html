The Andes: Southern Hemisphere ski season

This is the route for skiing in July. When it is summer up north it is winter in the Andes. You start at the high resorts in Chile near Santiago, then cross the mountains into Argentina for Bariloche, which has the biggest ski area in South America.

Route order: fly into Santiago, ski the Chilean resorts, then take the bus over the Andes to Bariloche and fly home through Buenos Aires.
Stop Nights Highlights
Santiago, Chile 3 Base city: Cerro San Cristóbal views, Chilean wine and the Mercado Central
Valle Nevado, Chile 8 High-altitude runs linked with La Parva and El Colorado; views of the Andes' tallest peaks; about an hour's drive from Santiago
Portillo, Chile 7 A historic resort on the shore of the frozen Laguna del Inca; steep, uncrowded chutes; one of the oldest ski areas in South America
Bariloche, Argentina 12 Cerro Catedral, the largest ski area in South America; Nahuel Huapi Lake; Argentine steak and famous chocolate shops
Total 30 4 stops

Where to stay

  • Santiago: hostels in Bellavista or Lastarria
  • Valle Nevado: on-mountain hotels; save money by day-tripping from Santiago by shuttle
  • Portillo: the Inca Lodge, the resort's budget bunk rooms
  • Bariloche: hostels in town, with buses to Cerro Catedral

At a glance

Best time: June to September. Mid-July is Argentine winter holiday, so expect crowds in Bariloche.

Daily budget: About $120–200 per person per day including lift passes.

Getting around: Ski shuttles from Santiago, then a long but scenic bus over the Andes to Bariloche.

Fly in / fly out: Arrive in Santiago (SCL) and leave from Bariloche (BRC).
