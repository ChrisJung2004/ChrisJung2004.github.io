Nature trips

These routes are for hiking, national parks, and wildlife. There is no skiing on these, just trails. Each route is built around a few big hikes or parks with a town in between so you can rest, restock, and do laundry.

Step 2: Pick a region

  • Nordic Countries Summer fjords and islands Iceland's Ring Road, Norwegian fjords, Lofoten Best time: Jun–Aug
  • Patagonia Glaciers and granite peaks Torres del Paine, El Chaltén, Perito Moreno Glacier Best time: Nov–Mar
  • The Himalayas Everest Base Camp, Annapurna, Ladakh Best time: Mar–May, Oct–Nov
  • North American National Parks Yosemite, Yellowstone, Banff, Jasper Best time: Jun–Sep
