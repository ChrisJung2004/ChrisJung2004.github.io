South America

Samba, tango, salsa, and reggaeton. This route goes to four of the best party cities in South America. If you can, plan it around Carnival in Rio. Then head south to Buenos Aires and up to Colombia for Medellín and Cartagena.

Route order: fly into Rio, then take flights south to Buenos Aires, north to Medellín, and a short hop to Cartagena before flying home.
Stop Nights Highlights
Rio de Janeiro, Brazil 9 Street parties under the Arcos da Lapa; Ipanema beach; Christ the Redeemer and Sugarloaf Mountain; Carnival samba parades at the Sambadrome
Buenos Aires, Argentina 8 Clubs that open after 1 a.m. in Palermo; a tango milonga in San Telmo; La Bomba de Tiempo drum show on Monday nights
Medellín, Colombia 8 Bars around Parque Lleras in El Poblado; salsa classes; the Comuna 13 graffiti tour; a day trip to Guatapé
Cartagena, Colombia 5 The walled Old City; street parties in Getsemaní; live salsa at Café Havana; a boat day to the Rosario Islands
Total 30 4 stops

Where to stay

  • Rio: hostels in Ipanema or Copacabana, close to the beach and the metro
  • Buenos Aires: hostels in Palermo, the nightlife district
  • Medellín: hostels in El Poblado, near Parque Lleras
  • Cartagena: hostels in Getsemaní, just outside the walls

At a glance

Best time: Any time of year. Carnival is in February or March, and prices in Rio triple that week.

Daily budget: About $50–90 per person per day. Rio during Carnival is the big exception.

Getting around: Flights between countries (LATAM, Avianca), metros and app taxis within cities.

Fly in / fly out: Arrive in Rio de Janeiro (GIG) and leave from Cartagena (CTG).
