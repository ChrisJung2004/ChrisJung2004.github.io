Mexico & Guatemala

This route has thousands of years of history and some of the best food in the world. You start in Mexico City, then eat your way through the moles and mezcal in Oaxaca. Then you climb Maya pyramids in the Yucatán, and finish in the jungle at Tikal in Guatemala.

Route order: fly into Mexico City, bus to Oaxaca, fly to Mérida, then travel by bus and shuttle to Flores, Guatemala, and fly home.
Stop Nights Highlights
Mexico City, Mexico 9 The Teotihuacan pyramids; the National Museum of Anthropology; tacos al pastor; the Frida Kahlo Museum in Coyoacán
Oaxaca, Mexico 7 Seven kinds of mole; mezcal tasting; the Zapotec ruins at Monte Albán; the mineral pools of Hierve el Agua
Mérida & Chichén Itzá, Mexico 7 El Castillo pyramid at Chichén Itzá; swimming in cenotes (natural sinkholes); cochinita pibil, the Yucatán's slow-roasted pork
Flores & Tikal, Guatemala 7 Sunrise over the Tikal temples with howler monkeys; the island town of Flores on Lake Petén Itzá
Total 30 4 stops

Where to stay

  • Mexico City: hostels in Roma or the Centro Histórico
  • Oaxaca: guesthouses in the city center, near the markets
  • Mérida: colonial-house hostels with courtyard pools
  • Flores: hostels on the island, with sunrise shuttles to Tikal

At a glance

Best time: November to April, the dry season. Day of the Dead (November 1–2) is spectacular in Oaxaca and Mexico City.

Daily budget: About $45–75 per person per day.

Getting around: ADO first-class buses in Mexico, a domestic flight to Mérida, and tourist shuttles into Guatemala.

Fly in / fly out: Arrive in Mexico City (MEX) and leave from Flores (FRS) or Guatemala City (GUA).
